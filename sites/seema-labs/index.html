01 / COMPANY

Why Seema Labs?

Autonomy is useful.
Authority should be intentional.

AI coding agents can modify software, run commands, access tools, and interact with development infrastructure. As that scope grows, organizations need a way to decide what authority to delegate.

Seema means “boundary” or “limit” in Nepali. We chose the name because our work focuses on defining clear boundaries for autonomous AI systems.

Seema Labs is built around a simple principle: AI agents should have enough freedom to do meaningful work while staying within clearly defined limits.

सीमाSEEMA / BOUNDARY, LIMIT

ROOTED IN NEPAL. FOCUSED ON WHAT COMES NEXT.

The story behind our name

We wanted a name that reflects both where we come from and the problem we are working on. “Seema” connects our Nepali roots to our belief that useful AI autonomy needs clear boundaries.

Visit Trinetra ↗
A PRODUCT FROM SEEMA LABS

Trinetra helps teams control what AI coding agents can access and change. It turns a coding task into focused permissions, explains why each permission is needed, and gives people control over approval.

  • Access relevant to the task at hand.
  • Human review and approval of proposed permissions.
  • Policy controls for sensitive files.
  • Clear reasons for requested access.
  • A review process for additional authority.
